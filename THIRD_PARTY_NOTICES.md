## Pelican Test

Version 2.0 also carries MIT-licensed Canvas drawing routines from the same twenty upstream scenes. Each entry in the [style catalogue](assets/medium/styles.json) lists its original file and operators. Science Motion Skills supplied subject-independent drawing rules and adapters, a scene API and player, and the new boat and cam-follower source examples. Generated HTML embeds the upstream and package licenses. [Pinned upstream code](https://github.com/orbitoo/pelican-test/tree/59c8ca2657ce5b2cf067969b5bda19d6609af8ac/src/scenes)

The 20-era sequence and selected procedural material routines and two-bone solver in the badminton showcase are adapted from **Elbert’s Pelican Test**, Copyright (c) 2026 Elbert, under the MIT License. The original film features a pelican riding a bicycle. Science Motion Skills created the robot drawings, badminton choreography, shuttle path, hit sounds and single-file viewer for this adaptation.
